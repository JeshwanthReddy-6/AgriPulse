# AgriPulse
Many farmers struggle to identify crop diseases and choose the right pesticides for treatment, often relying on guesswork or costly advice. 🚜
Every year, this lack of awareness leads to nearly 40% of global crop yield losses due to pests and preventable diseases.

AgriPulse was built to change that — empowering farmers to protect their crops and profits through AI-driven insights, early disease detection, and multilingual guidance. 🌾
By blending intelligence with accessibility, it transforms traditional farming into a smarter, data-driven experience that brings expert-level support right to the farmer’s hands. 💬✨

✨ Core Features:
📸 AI Disease Detection
🤖 Smart Chatbot
🌾 Farmer Hub
📊 Yield & Soil Analysis
🚜 AgriTech Awareness
